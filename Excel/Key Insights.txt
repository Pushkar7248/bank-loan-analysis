Key Insights

1. Data Cleaning: Identified and handled inconsistent entries, missing values, duplicate records, and formatting issues to prepare the dataset for accurate analysis.

2. Loan Portfolio Overview: Used Excel formulas to calculate important KPIs, including total loans, total loan amount, average loan amount, and customer-related statistics.

3. Loan Status Analysis: Used PivotTables to examine different loan statuses and understand the distribution of Fully Paid, Current, Late, and Charged Off loans.

4. Customer Analysis: Analysed customer information to identify borrowing patterns across different age groups, genders, and income categories.

5. Loan Purpose Analysis: Compared loan amounts and application counts across different loan purposes to understand customer borrowing preferences.

6. KPI Dashboard: Created a summary dashboard using formulas, PivotTables, and charts to present important loan performance indicators in an organised format.
